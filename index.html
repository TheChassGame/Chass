How to play Chass

A quick guide to setup, turns, pieces, points, and winning.

Goal

Checkmate the opposing king. A king in check must be made safe. If it cannot escape check by a legal move, a legal purchase, or promotion, the game is checkmate. You may also resign; the other side wins. The game is an automatic draw if the same sequence of moves and purchases by both sides is repeated three times. Skips are ignored, and point totals do not matter.

Start the game

Choose 2 Player or vs Engine before starting; the mode locks once king placement begins. The 4 × 4 board starts empty. White places its king on any empty square on White’s back rank, then Black places its king on any empty square on Black’s back rank. White takes the first turn.

One action per turn

  • Move: move one of your pieces by its normal chess movement rules.
  • Buy: spend points on a piece and place it on any empty square in your own half of the board.
  • Skip: gain 2 points, up to the 12-point bank limit. You cannot skip while in check or when your bank already has 12 points.

Points and shop prices

Each player starts with 5 points. A pawn costs 1, a knight 3, a bishop 3, a rook 5, and a queen 10. Captures do not award points; skipping is how you earn points. You cannot spend more than you have or hold more than 12 points.

Piece limits

  • Up to 3 pawns per side, with no two same-color pawns on the same file.
  • Up to 2 knights, 2 bishops, and 2 rooks per side.
  • Up to 1 queen per side.
  • Promotion may exceed the pawn, knight, bishop, or rook limits. The queen limit still applies.

Movement, check, and special rules

Moves must follow standard chess movement and leave your own king safe. Your king may move only while it is in check, and then only to a safe square. Castling, standard en passant, and the pawn's two-square first move are not used. Pawns move one square forward and capture diagonally as usual. En voyant: if you buy a pawn directly beside one or more opposing pawns on the same rank, an adjacent pawn may capture it on its very next turn by moving one square diagonally forward into the empty square beyond it. Remove the bought pawn; only one adjacent pawn can make the capture, and the chance expires after any other action. The capture must leave your king safe. The en voyant capture uses ordinary capture notation in the history. A purchase made while in check must resolve the check.

Promotion

When a pawn reaches the farthest rank, promote it to a queen, rook, bishop, or knight, subject to the queen limit. The promotion menu shows the available choices.

Notation and game tools

Moves appear in the history using chess notation. King placement is written like ka1; a skip is //; a purchase is written as piece/square, such as N/a3. Use Engine suggestion to see the top action and its best line, or switch to vs Engine to play White against Black’s engine. After a finished game, Review game rates the actions and shows a suggested best action.
